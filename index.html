WHAT IS PHYSICAL AI?

It is the intersection of artificial intelligence and the physical world—where models don't just generate text, but parse complex sensor data to actuate and protect human life in real-time.

WHY FOCUS ON THE EDGE?

Because latency costs lives. When navigating high-density roads, a 200ms cloud round-trip is unacceptably dangerous. Intelligence must live at the sensor level.
